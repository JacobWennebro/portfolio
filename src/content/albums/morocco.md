Two weeks bouncing between cities by train. Morocco moves at its own pace — the medinas are chaotic in the best way, and the food alone was worth the trip.

Chefchaouen was the highlight. An entire town painted blue, tucked into the Rif Mountains. We spent a full day just getting lost in the alleys.

The Sahara was surreal. Camels at sunrise, silence you can feel, stars brighter than anywhere I've been.
